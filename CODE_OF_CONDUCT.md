# Code of conduct

OpenXER follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep
discussion about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not
accepted in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the
repository's Security tab (**Report a vulnerability** also reaches them
privately), or by a direct message to a maintainer. Reports are handled in
confidence, and the maintainers may remove comments or block participants
who break this code.
