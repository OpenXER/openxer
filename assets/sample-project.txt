# The sample project a new workspace starts on (xer/src/sample_project.rs).
# Everything here is invented: the plant, the people, the vendors, the
# quantities, the rates and the dates. Fields are separated by " | ".
#
# Durations are working days of the activity's calendar: 5 is the 5-day
# 8-hour office calendar, 6 the 6-day 10-hour construction calendar and 7 the
# 7-day 10-hour calendar for concrete curing and the plant outage.
# A duration of "start" or "finish" makes a milestone, "loe" makes a Level of
# Effort activity, and "wbs" makes a WBS Summary activity.
# Predecessors: ID (finish to start), "ID SS2" (start to start, 2 days lag on
# the predecessor's calendar), "ID FF1", "ID FS1".
# Resources: "ID units" where units is the crew size (units per hour) for
# labor and nonlabor resources and the total quantity for materials. No
# value may hold a "|".
