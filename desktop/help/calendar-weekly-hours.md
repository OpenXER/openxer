# Calendar Weekly Hours

Sets the normal working week of a calendar: the hours of each weekday, Sunday to Saturday. Every date follows its weekday's hours unless it is an exception, such as a holiday. Open it with the **Workweek...** button of the [calendar editor](calendar-editor).

## What is on it
- **Standard work hours**: the seven weekdays, **Sun** to **Sat**.
- **The day boxes** (total work hours/day mode): type the working hours of each weekday, from 0 to 24, rounded to the half hour. 0 makes that weekday nonwork.
- **The day captions** (detailed work hours/day mode): click a weekday to select it, or `Ctrl`+click to add or remove days. Monday is selected when the dialog opens. Each day's total hours show under its caption.
- **Work** (detailed mode): gives the selected nonwork weekdays a standard 8 hour day, 08:00 to 12:00 and 13:00 to 17:00.
- **Nonwork** (detailed mode): makes the selected weekdays nonwork.
- **The half hour grid** (detailed mode): shows the first selected weekday. Click a half hour to switch it between work and nonwork on all the selected weekdays.
- **OK**: keeps the new week in the calendar editor.
- **Cancel**: drops the changes.

The mode follows the **Total work hours/day** or **Detailed work hours/day** choice in the calendar editor.

## Common tasks
1. In the calendar editor, click **Workweek...**.
2. For a six day week, type 8 in the **Sat** box.
3. For four 10 hour days, type 10 for Monday to Thursday and 0 for Friday.
4. Click **OK**, then **OK** in the calendar editor, and press `F9`.

## Good to know
> In total mode a day becomes one unbroken period that keeps its start time and runs for the hours you type, starting at 08:00 if it was nonwork. Use detailed mode to set a lunch break or a night shift.
> Dates that now match their weekday's hours stop being exceptions.
> The week is saved only when you click **OK** in the calendar editor as well.
