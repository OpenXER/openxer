# Chart Font and Colors

Sets the font and colors of the Gantt chart: its text, background, the shading of nonwork time and the colors of the sight lines. Choose `View > Chart Font and Colors...`.

## What is on it
- **Font**: the sample button shows the font used for bar labels. Click it to choose the **Font**, **Font style** (Regular or Bold), **Size** (6 to 20) and **Color** in the **Font** window.
- **Background**: the color behind the bars. Click the swatch to pick a color.
- **Shade nonwork time**: when ticked, days and hours that are not work time on the calendar (such as weekends) are shaded. The swatch next to it sets the shade color.
- **Sight Lines**: the colors of the guide lines that help your eye follow the chart.
  - **Horizontal major** and **Horizontal minor**: lines across the chart.
  - **Vertical major** and **Vertical minor**: lines down the chart at set time steps.
  Use each swatch to set that line's color. Whether each line shows is set in [Bar Chart Options](bar-chart-options), on the Sight Lines tab.
- **OK**: applies and closes. **Apply** applies and stays open. **Cancel** closes and changes nothing.
- **Default**: puts back the standard font, colors, shading and sight lines.
- **Help**: opens this topic.

## Common tasks
1. Choose `View > Chart Font and Colors...`.
2. Tick **Shade nonwork time** so weekends stand out.
3. Click the **Vertical major** swatch and pick a darker color.
4. Click **OK**.
5. To show the vertical major lines, right-click the Gantt chart, choose **Bar Chart Options...**, and on **Sight Lines** tick **Show Major Lines every** under **Vertical Lines**.

## Good to know
> The bar colors themselves are set in [Bars](bars), not here.
> **Shade nonwork time** here and **Shade calendar nonwork time** in Bar Chart Options are the same setting.
> These settings belong to the current layout and are kept in it when you save it.
