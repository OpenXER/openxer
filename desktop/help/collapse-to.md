# Collapse To

Folds a grouped activity table to one outline level, so you see only the band rows down to that level. Choose `View > Collapse To...`, or right-click the table and choose **Collapse To...**.

## What is on it
- **Band level to collapse to**: a drop-down of the band levels in the table, from the top down, each named by what it groups by, such as "WBS Level 2" or the name of an activity code. It is blank until you pick one.
- **OK**: shows levels 1 to the picked level and folds the bands of that level, hiding everything under them, then closes. With no level picked it just closes.
- **Cancel**: closes without changing anything.
- **Apply**: folds to the picked level and keeps the window open, so you can try another level.
- **Help**: opens this topic.

## Common tasks
1. Group the table first, for example by WBS (the work breakdown of the project) in [Group and Sort](group-and-sort).
2. Choose `View > Collapse To...`.
3. Pick the second level in the drop-down and click **OK**. The table shows the top two levels of bands, with their activities hidden.
4. Click the expand box on any band row (or double-click the band) to open just that band, or use `View > Expand All` to see everything again.

## Good to know
> Collapse To needs bands to work on. If the table is not grouped, the drop-down is empty, **Apply** is off, and the status bar says "Collapse To needs grouping bands (Group and Sort)."
> Collapsing only changes what is shown. No activity is changed or removed.
> `View > Collapse All` folds every band at once; `View > Expand All` opens them all.
