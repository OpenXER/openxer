# Customize Activity Details

Chooses which tabs show in the Activity Details pane under the activity table,
and in what order. Right-click the tab strip of the pane and choose
**Customize Activity Details...**, or choose `View > Bottom Layout Options...`
while the bottom pane shows Activity Details. With a usage spreadsheet or usage profile
in the bottom pane, `View > Bottom Layout Options...` opens that view's own
**Spreadsheet Options** or **Profile Options** instead, the same windows its
right-click menu offers.

## What is on it
- **Available Tabs**: tabs that are hidden now.
- **Display Tabs**: tabs that show, top to bottom in the order they appear left to
  right in the pane.
- **>** moves the tab picked in Available Tabs to the end of Display Tabs.
  **>>** moves them all. **<** moves the picked display tab back to Available
  Tabs. **<<** hides every tab except the first.
- **^** and **v** move the picked display tab up or down.
- **OK** keeps the choice and closes; **Apply** keeps it and stays open;
  **Cancel** closes without changes.
- **Default** puts back the standard set of tabs: **General**, **Status**,
  **Resources**, **Predecessors** and **Successors**.
- **Help**: opens this topic.

## Common tasks
1. Click a tab name in **Available Tabs** and click **>** to show it.
2. Pick it in **Display Tabs** and use **^** or **v** to move it where you want it.
3. Click **OK**.

## Good to know
> At least one tab always stays on display: **<** is off when only one tab is
  left, and **<<** keeps the first tab.
> The choice is kept in the workspace, not in the project or the layout, so it
  stays when you open another project.
> To hide the whole pane, right-click its tab strip and choose
  **Hide Detail Window**.
