# Customize

Chooses which buttons each toolbar shows. Choose `View > Toolbars > Customize...`, right-click a toolbar and choose **Customize...**, or click the small arrow at the end of a toolbar and choose **Customize...**.

## What is on it
- **Toolbars**: every toolbar, such as **Standard**, **Edit**, **Layout**, **Tools**, **Find**, **Print** and **Reports**. Click one to see its buttons.
- **Buttons**: the buttons of the picked toolbar. Tick a button to show it on the bar; clear it to hide it. The toolbar changes at once.
- **Reset**: shows every button of the picked toolbar again, the way it came.
- **Close**: closes the window. `Escape` does the same.

## Common tasks
To keep the Tools toolbar short:
1. Choose `View > Toolbars > Customize...`.
2. Click **Tools** in the **Toolbars** list.
3. Clear the buttons you never use, such as **Progress Spotlight**.
4. Click **Close**.

To show a whole toolbar that is hidden, tick it on the `View > Toolbars` menu instead.

## Related commands on the View menu
- `View > Toolbars`: tick a toolbar name to show or hide the whole bar.
- `View > Toolbars > Reset Toolbars`: puts every bar back at its starting place.
- `View > Reset All Toolbars`: goes further. It shows the standard toolbars with every button, hides the optional ones, and puts every bar back at its starting place.
- `View > Lock All Toolbars`: hides the drag grips so the bars cannot be moved by accident.

## Good to know
> There is no OK or Cancel: each tick or clear takes effect straight away, so Close keeps what you did.
> Your toolbar choices are saved in the workspace and come back next time OpenXER starts.
> Hiding a button only removes it from the bar. The same command stays on its menu and keeps its shortcut key.
> These settings never change the project.
