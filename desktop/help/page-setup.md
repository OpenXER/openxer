# Page Setup

Sets how the current layout prints: paper, scaling, margins, header, footer and what goes on the page. Choose `File > Page Setup...`. It is available in the Activities, WBS, Projects, Resources and Resource Assignments views. The settings are saved in the workspace for the current layout (in the other views, for that view).

## What is on it
- **Page** tab: **Orientation** (**Portrait**, **Landscape** or **Automatic**). **Scaling**: **Adjust to** 10 to 400 percent of normal size, **Fit to** a number of pages wide by tall (0 tall leaves the height free), or **Fit timescale to** a number of pages wide. **Allow pages side by side** lets a printout be more than one page wide; without it, pages wide stays at 1. A line under Scaling says how the result will print; when the text comes out small it offers **Use Landscape** or a larger paper. **Paper size** picks the sheet; its width and height are shown.
- **Margins** tab: **Top**, **Left**, **Right** and **Bottom**, in inches from 0 to 4.
- **Header** and **Footer** tabs: **Divide Into** 1 to 5 sections, **Include on** first, last, all or no pages, and the band **Height** in inches. Drag the handles on the ruler to resize sections. Click a section and pick what it holds: nothing, text, the chart legend or a revision box. For text, type it, add codes from **Insert field...** (project ID, data date, page number and more; each goes at the end of the text) and choose Left, Center or Right. **Copy** copies the text to the clipboard. **Show Section Divider Lines** draws lines between sections.
- **Options** tab: **Timescale Start** and **Timescale Finish** for the printed chart, or tick **Same as screen**. Type a date or a code such as DD-1W or PF+1W, or use the `...` button: a small picker where you choose what the date counts from (today, data date, project start, project finish, start of this week or month, or a specific date) and an offset in days, weeks, months or years. It shows the date that results. **Print** chooses the parts: **Activity Table**, **All Columns**, **Grid Lines**, **Gantt Chart**, and the bottom pane when it shows a profile, spreadsheet or trace logic. Also **Break Page Every Group** and **Repeat band header on each page** (only when the layout is grouped) and **Relationship Lines** (only with Gantt Chart ticked).
- **OK** saves and closes; **Apply** saves and stays open (available once something changed); **Default** puts back the starting values, saving nothing until OK or Apply; **Cancel** closes without saving.

## Common tasks
1. Choose `File > Page Setup...`.
2. On **Page**, pick **Landscape** and **Fit to** 1 page wide.
3. On **Header**, set a section to text and add **Project Name** from **Insert field...**.
4. Click **OK**, then check the result in `File > Print Preview`.

## Good to know
> A Print Preview that is open refreshes when you click OK or Apply.
