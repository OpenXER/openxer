# Print Preview

Shows the printed pages of the current view before you print, exactly as they will come out. Choose `File > Print Preview`. It is available in the same views as [Page Setup](page-setup). Reports open in the same viewer.

## What is on it
- **Page Setup**: opens [Page Setup](page-setup). The preview refreshes when you click OK or Apply there.
- **Print**: makes a PDF of the pages and opens it in your PDF viewer, where you print it.
- **Publish to PDF**: asks for a file name and saves the pages there as a PDF.
- **Pages**: shows or hides a strip of small page pictures. Click one to jump to that page.
- **Find**: opens a find bar. Type a word and press `Enter`; **Previous** and **Next** step through the matches, and the bar shows how many there are.
- **Zoom Out**, **Zoom In** and the zoom box: **Fit Width**, **Fit Page**, 50%, 75%, 100%, 150% or 200%.
- **Zoom to Fit**: shows the whole page.
- **First Page**, **Previous Page**, **Next Page**, **Last Page**, and the **Page** box to type a page number. When the printout runs more than one page across, the arrows step left, right, up and down one page.
- **Close**: closes the preview.
- The status line at the bottom gives the page count and the print scale.

## Common tasks
1. Choose `File > Print Preview`.
2. Read the status line. If it is orange, the text prints small or the pages run side by side.
3. Click **Page Setup** to change paper, orientation or scaling.
4. Click **Print** or **Publish to PDF**.

## Keys
- `Page Up` and `Page Down` move a page; `Home` and `End` go to the first and last page.
- The arrow keys scroll a little at a time. The mouse wheel and a drag on the page scroll too.
- `Ctrl+F` opens the find bar.

## Good to know
> "pages across: tape or place side by side" means the printout is wider than one sheet. Turn off side-by-side pages or fit to one page wide in Page Setup.
> The zoom and page strip settings are kept for next time.
