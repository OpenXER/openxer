# Report Groups

Report groups are folders for your saved reports, so a long list stays easy to find. This dialog builds the group tree and moves reports between groups. Open it with `Tools > Reports > Report Groups...` or the Report Groups button on the Reports toolbar.

## What is on it
- **Report Group / Report** list: the groups as a tree with their reports under them. **Subject Area** shows what a Report Wizard report lists, such as Wizard: Activities. Reports built in the Report Editor or imported show Definition, and reports supplied with OpenXER show OpenXER.
- **Report Group Name** (when a group is selected): type a new name. It is saved as you type.
- **Report Group** (when a report is selected): a drop-down that moves the report to another group, or to **(none)**.
- **Close**: closes the dialog.
- **Add**: adds a group named New Report Group under the selected group (or under the group of the selected report).
- **Delete**: removes the selected group. Its sub-groups and reports move up one level.
- **Cut**, **Copy**, **Paste**: move or duplicate a group with its sub-groups. Cut and Paste moves the group and its reports. Copy and Paste makes new, empty groups: the reports are not copied. Paste puts the group under the selected group (or the group of the selected report), or at the top level when nothing is selected.
- **Move Up**, **Move Down**: change the group's place among its neighbours.
- **Move Left**, **Move Right**: move the group out one level, or into the group just above it at the same level, as its last sub-group.
- **Help**: opens this topic.

## Common tasks
1. Click **Add**, then type the group's name in **Report Group Name**.
2. Select a report in the list and pick the new group in **Report Group**.
3. Use the move buttons to arrange the tree.

## Good to know
> A top-level group that still holds reports cannot be deleted. Move or delete its reports first.
> Delete, Cut, Copy and the move buttons work on groups only, not on reports.
> Every change is saved as you make it; there is no OK or Cancel.
> Report groups are kept in your workspace, not in the project, so they are the same for every project.
