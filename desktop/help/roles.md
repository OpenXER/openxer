# Roles

A role is a job or skill, such as pipefitter or electrician, that you can plan with before you name a specific person or crew. This dialog keeps the tree of roles, their prices and their role codes. Open it with `Enterprise > Roles...`. It also opens from a resource's role picker, with a **Select** button added.

## What is on it
- **Role ID**, **Role Name** list: the tree of roles. Click a cell of the selected row to edit it in place. The ID must be unique in the whole tree; the name must be unique among roles at the same level.
- **General** tab: **Role ID**, **Role Name** and **Responsibilities** (free notes) for the selected role.
- **Prices** tab: **Price / Unit** to **Price / Unit5**, five price rates for the role. A price cannot be negative.
- **Codes** tab: one row per role code with this role's **Code Value**. Click a row, then pick a value from the drop-down in its Code Value cell, or the blank entry to clear it.
- **Close**, **Select** (only when opened from a picker: puts the selected role on the resource and closes), **Add** (adds under the selected role), **Delete**, **Cut**, **Copy**, **Paste**, the **arrow pad** and **Help**.
- Right-click a row for Add, Delete, Cut, Copy, Paste and **Role Details**, which shows the General tab.

## Common tasks
1. Select a parent role and click **Add**.
2. Fill in **Role ID** and **Role Name** on the General tab.
3. Enter rates on the **Prices** tab.
4. Assign code values on the **Codes** tab.

## Good to know
> Deleting a role also deletes the roles under it with their prices and code values, and removes them from any assignments and resources that use them, after you confirm.
> Role codes and their values are set up in Role Codes, not here.
