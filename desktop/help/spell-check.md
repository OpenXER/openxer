# Spell Check

Checks the spelling of the open project's activity names, step names and notebook notes against a built-in English word list and your own dictionary. It starts at the selected activity and goes through every activity once. Choose `Edit > Spell Check...` or press `F7`.

## What is on it
- **The top lines**: which text the word is in (for example an activity name), and the word shown in its sentence.
- **Not in Dictionary**: the word that was not found.
- **Change To**: the word to put in its place. Type it, or pick from the list below.
- **Suggestions**: up to eight words close to the one found; the first is already in **Change To**. Click one to put it in **Change To**, or double-click it to change the word at once.
- **Ignore**: leaves this one as it is and moves on.
- **Ignore All**: leaves the word as it is everywhere for the rest of this check.
- **Change**: replaces this one with **Change To**.
- **Change All**: replaces this one, and the same word wherever it turns up for the rest of this check.
- **Add**: adds the word to your own dictionary, so it is never flagged again. Good for tag numbers, place names and trade words.
- **Close**: ends the check and makes the changes.
- **Cancel**: ends the check and drops every change made in it. The project is left as it was.
- **Help**: opens this topic.

When nothing is left to check, the window says "Spell checking complete."

## Common tasks
1. Press `F7`.
2. For each word, click **Change** to accept the suggestion, **Ignore** to skip it, or **Add** if it is a real word your crews use.
3. When the window says the check is complete, click **Close**.

## Good to know
> The table moves to the activity that holds each word, so you can see it in place.
> Changes are held until you click **Close**, then go into the project together as one undo step; use `Ctrl+Z` afterwards to take them back. The status bar says how many words changed.
> **Cancel**, the close box and `Escape` drop the held changes and leave the project as it was. Words you added to your own dictionary stay added.
> Your own dictionary is kept in the workspace and used for every project. It is not written to the XER.
> The built-in word list is English.
