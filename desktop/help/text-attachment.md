# Text Attachment

Adds a note in a text box on the Gantt chart, pinned to a date and a row, or changes one that is there. To add one, choose `View > Attachments > Text`, or right-click the chart and choose `Attachments > Text`, then click the chart where the box should go. To change one, double-click or right-click the text box.

## What is on it
- **Text**: the words to show, up to 2000 characters. Press `Enter` for a new line.
- **Font**: the sample button shows the font. Click it to choose the font, style, size (6 to 20 points) and color in the **Font** window.
- **Color**: the color of the text. Click the swatch to pick a color.
- **OK**: places or updates the text box. A box needs some text; with none, a message shows and the window stays open.
- **Cancel** or `Escape`: closes without changing the chart.
- **Delete**: removes the text box. It is available only for a box that is already on the chart.
- **Help**: opens this topic.

## Common tasks
1. Choose `View > Attachments > Text`. The status bar says "Click the chart where the text box goes. Escape cancels."
2. Click the chart at the date and row the note is about, for example beside a shutdown milestone. The date is rounded to the hour.
3. Type the note, such as "Unit down for tie-ins".
4. Click **OK**.

To move a text box, drag it on the chart. It takes the date and row where you drop it.

## Good to know
> Press `Escape` instead of clicking the chart to cancel placing a new box.
> The text box belongs to the layout, not to the project: it is kept in the workspace and saved with the layout. It is not part of the undo history, and it is not written to the XER file.
> The box is tied to the row you clicked, so it moves with that activity or band when the table is sorted. While that row is filtered out or collapsed, the box is not shown.
> `View > Attachments` is available in the Activities view.
