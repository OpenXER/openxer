# Activities

The Activities view is where you build and update the schedule: a table of activities on the left, a Gantt chart on the right and the Activity Details tabs at the bottom. Open it with `Project > Activities` or the **Activities** button on the Project bar.

## What it shows
- **Layout bar**: the strip above the table. Click **Layout:** for layout files and display settings such as **Bars...**, **Columns...**, **Timescale...**, **Filters...** and **Show on Bottom**. Click **Filter:** to open the Filters dialog.
- **Usage profiles** in the bottom pane (**Show on Bottom**): right-click the Activity Usage Profile or Resource Usage Profile and choose **Stacked Histogram** to split each bar by resource, or under **Stack By** by role. Each series gets its own color and the legend names it. **Show Actual and Remaining** (on the same menu, and in **Profile Options...**) stands each period's actual and remaining units in one bar, actual at the bottom; off, they are two bars side by side. With **Stacked Histogram** on as well, each series' block is split: its actual units at the bottom in a darker shade of its color, its remaining units above. Each profile keeps its own choices, saved with the layout.
- **Resource list** of the Resource Usage Profile and Spreadsheet: the **Display:** bar over it filters it (the current project's resources by default, all resources, or a custom filter), groups and sorts it (hierarchy, list, primary role, resource type or resource codes), picks its columns, and switches to roles. Click a column header to sort. Ctrl+click and Shift+click select several resources and show their sum. The **Resource** and **Time Period** boxes under the list filter the activity table; the **Filter:** caption then says so, with **Clear** at its end. See [Usage Spreadsheets and Profiles](usage-views).
- **Activity table**: one row per activity, grouped in bands (by WBS unless you change the grouping). Dates that are actuals end in ` A`. A start or finish held by a constraint (a date you fixed on the activity) ends in ` *`. An actual date shows only ` A`, never both. The marks are for display only; you can type a date with or without them.
- **Gantt chart**: a bar per activity on a timescale, with relationship lines between bars. Right-click a bar, the empty chart or the timescale for the chart menu (below); zoom with `View > Zoom`.
- **Activity Details**: tabs for the selected activity, such as **General**, **Status**, **Resources**, **Codes**, **Relationships**, **Notebook**, **Steps** and **Notes to Resources**. Right-click the tab strip for **Hide Detail Window** or **Customize Activity Details...** to pick and order the tabs. On the **General** tab, the **...** of the WBS box opens **Select WBS**; pick an element and click **Assign** (or double-click it) to move the activity there. The buttons that add something to a tab (**Assign**, **Add**, **Add from Template**, **Add Role**) are always available; when there is nothing to choose, their list opens empty. On **Notes to Resources**, **Add** with nothing typed puts the cursor in the empty box above it.

## What you can do here
- Press `Insert` to add an activity, `Delete` to delete the selected ones, and `F2` (or just start typing) to edit the focused cell.
- **Add** opens the [New Activity Wizard](new-activity-wizard) unless it is turned off in [User Preferences](user-preferences), Assistance. Without the wizard, **Add** puts the new activity in the WBS of the selected activity and opens its name for typing, with "New Activity" selected: type the name and press Enter. It takes the project's **Defaults** tab values (Projects view): Activity Type, Duration Type, Percent Complete Type and Calendar, with an Original and Remaining Duration of 5 days. Its Activity ID comes from the Auto-numbering Defaults: with **Increment Activity ID based on selected activity** ticked and an activity selected, the selected ID plus the Increment (stepping on past IDs in use, so a gap is filled first); otherwise the highest ID with the Prefix plus the Increment, or Prefix and Suffix in a project that has none yet. A selected ID that does not end in a number counts as nothing selected.
- Type a new **Start** for an activity that has not started (in the Start cell or on the **Status** tab) and OpenXER asks whether to keep the activity on that date with a Start On or After constraint. **Yes** moves Start and Planned Start with the constraint, so scheduling keeps it there; **No** moves them with no constraint, so the next schedule (F9) may move it back. The finish follows by the duration either way, and `Ctrl+Z` undoes it in one step. Escape leaves the activity as it was. Tick **Always use this answer** to stop the question; [User Preferences](user-preferences), **Application** tab, brings it back. A typed **Finish** never asks: the start stays and the Original and Remaining Duration change to fit.
- A new activity (Add or Paste) starts at the data date on its calendar and finishes after its duration, so its bar shows at once. Its late dates and float stay blank until you schedule (F9).
- Right-click a row, a group band or a column title (they share one menu) for **Cut**, **Copy**, **Paste**, **Fill Down** (`Ctrl+E`), **Add**, **Delete**, **Dissolve** (removes an activity and links its predecessors to its successors), **Renumber Activity IDs**, **Export to Excel**, **Assign**, and **Link Activities** or **Unlink Activities** for two or more selected rows.
- To move activities to another WBS element, **Cut** them (`Ctrl+X`), select an activity or the band of the target element and **Paste** (`Ctrl+V`). Cut asks nothing and leaves the rows where they are until you paste; the moved activities keep their IDs, relationships and everything else, with no dialogs, and `Ctrl+Z` puts them back. Pasting into the WBS they are already in changes nothing. A later Copy or Cut replaces the cut. Pasted on an activity of another open project, cut activities move there the same way: they leave their own project, and a relationship with an activity left behind becomes a relationship between the two projects (one that cannot be kept, as between two projects with the same project ID, is removed and the status bar says how many). `Ctrl+Z` undoes the move in both projects at once. If that project already has one of their Activity IDs, nothing moves and the cut stays, so you can renumber and paste again. If their own project has been closed, the cut activities are copied instead, through Copy Activity Options. **Copy** then **Paste** always makes new activities.
- Drag from the start or finish end of a bar onto another activity's start or finish to create a relationship. `Escape` cancels the drag.
- Drag the body of a not-started bar to move its start, or the strip just inside its finish end to change its duration.
- Right-click the chart for **Activity Details**, **Bars...**, **Bar Chart Options...**, **Timescale...** and **Attachments** (curtains that shade a date range, and text boxes).

## Good to know
> `Project > Additional Activities View` opens a second Activities tab on the same project with its own layout, selection and scroll. An edit in either one shows in both and goes into the same undo history.
> On the **Status** tab, the **At Complete** box can be typed for units and for costs (pick the figure with the chooser above the boxes). The remaining figure becomes the new value less the actual; on an activity that has not started (with budget and remaining linked in the project) the budget follows too, and a cost goes to the units at the price while costs and units are linked. It cannot go below the actual figure. **Actual** is read only until the activity starts, **Remaining** and **At Complete** once it is completed.
> The **Resources** tab can show more columns (right-click the assignments, **Customize Resource Columns...**): **Budgeted Cost**, **Actual Cost**, **Remaining Cost** and **At Completion Cost**, which you can type (with costs linked to units, the units follow at the price), **At Completion Units** (typed, the remaining units take it less the actual), **Start**, **Finish**, **Actual Start** and **Actual Finish** (a date box whatever the status; a date the status does not allow is refused), **Planned Start** and **Planned Finish**, **Units This Period** and **Cost This Period** (typed once the activity has started, and filled by Apply Actuals and Update Progress), and the **Cost Units Linked** tick box, which makes the assignment's remaining cost follow its units at its price. Ticking it again asks first, since the costs are worked out again. **Rate Source** is a drop-down: a price that comes from the resource cannot be typed; choose **Override** to type one.
> Started and completed bars cannot be dragged to new dates, and neither can level of effort, WBS summary and finish milestone bars. While more than one project is open, no bar can be dragged.
> Dropping a moved bar asks the same question as typing a Start, and **Always use this answer** covers both. The new start keeps the time of day where you dropped the bar (to the hour). Stretching the start end holds the new start with a Start On or After constraint without asking; stretching the finish end adds no constraint.
> **Dissolve** (`Edit > Dissolve` or the row menu) is available only for activities with at least one predecessor and at least one successor, and asks before it runs. It removes the activity and ties every predecessor to every successor. Each new relationship starts where the predecessor relationship started (the predecessor's finish for FS and FF, its start for SS and SF) and ends where the successor relationship ended (the successor's start for FS and SS, its finish for FF and SF): A SS B with B FF D becomes A SF D. Two activities that are already related keep the relationship they have. The new lag is 0 unless **Retain Lag** is on in [User Preferences](user-preferences), Calculations. Dates do not move until you schedule (F9); `Ctrl+Z` brings the activity and its relationships back in one step.
