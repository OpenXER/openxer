# Issues

The Issues view lists the open project's issues: problems you are tracking until they are resolved. An issue can be raised by hand, or by `Tools > Monitor Thresholds...` when an activity or WBS element crosses a threshold. Open it with `Project > Issues`.

## What it shows
- **Display:** the strip above the table. Click it to show **All Issues**, **Open Issues** (On Hold included) or **Closed Issues**, to **Expand All** or **Collapse All**, and to show or hide the details pane with **Details**.
- **Issue table**: one row per issue with **Issue**, **Priority**, **Status**, **Responsible Manager**, **Date Identified**, **Identified By**, **Resolution Date**, **Activity ID**, **WBS**, **Threshold Parameter**, **Actual Value**, **Lower Threshold** and **Upper Threshold**.
- **Command bar**: **Add** and **Delete** on the right edge.
- **Issue Details**: tabs for the selected issue.
- **General**: the issue name, **Responsible OBS** (the person or team in charge), priority, status (**Open**, **On Hold** or **Closed**), who identified it, and the identified and resolution dates.
- **Details**: what the issue applies to. **Activity...** and **WBS...** pick an activity or WBS element, and **Applies to Resource** picks a resource. For an issue raised by a threshold, the parameter, the lower and upper limits and the actual value are shown read-only.
- **Notes**: free text, with the **Issue History** shown read-only below it when the file carries any.
- **User Defined Fields**.

## Common tasks
1. Click **Add** (or press `Insert`). A new issue starts Open with Normal priority, identified today.
2. Name it and set the **Responsible OBS** and priority on the **General** tab.
3. On **Details**, link it to the activity or WBS element it is about.
4. When it is settled, set **Status** to Closed and enter the **Resolution Date**.

Right-click a row for **Add**, **Delete**, **Issue Details** and **Hide Detail Window**.

## Good to know
> **Customize Columns...** on the right-click menu picks and orders the columns; the choice is kept with the view layout.
> Deleting an issue also deletes its history.
> **Tracking Layout** on the Details tab is not available in this build.
> `Tools > Issue Navigator...` lists the same issues in a window of its own.
