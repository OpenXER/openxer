# Reports

The Reports view lists your saved reports, filed under their report groups, and is where you run, change and organize them. Open it with `Tools > Reports > Reports` or the **Reports** button on the Enterprise bar.

## What it shows
- **Display:** the strip above the table. Click it to filter (**All Reports**, **Global Reports**, **Project Reports**, **Wizard Reports**, **Editor Reports**), group, sort, show the **Report Group Column**, or expand and collapse the bands.
- **Report table**: one row per report with **Report Name**, **Report Scope** (Global for every project, or Project for one) and **Last Run Date**. Reports sit under blue bands for their groups; reports in no group sit under **No Report Group**. The icon by a name shows whether the report was made with the Report Wizard or the report editor.
- **Reports toolbar**: **Run Report**, **Run Batch**, **Modify Report**, **Import Report**, **Export Report**, **Report Wizard**, **Report Groups** and **Batch Reports**. It starts hidden; turn it on with `View > Toolbars > Reports`.

## What you can do here
- Double-click a report to run it.
- Right-click a report for **Run** (**Report**, **Report to File...**, **Batch...**), **Modify...**, **Import...**, **Export...**, **Add...** (starts the Report Wizard), **Add Blank Report...** (opens an empty report in the report editor), **Delete**, **Cut**, **Copy**, **Paste**, **Report Scope**, **Report Group...** and **Display Options**.

## The Report Wizard
Open it with **Add...**, the **Report Wizard** button or `Tools > Report Wizard...`. Move through its pages with **Prev** and **Next**:
1. Start: choose **New Report**, **Use Current Screen** (copies the Activities layout) or **Modify Wizard Report**.
2. **Subject Area**: Activities, Resources, Resource Assignments or Relationships.
3. **Columns, Filter, Group and Sort**: move fields from **Available Options** to **Selected Options** with **Add >**, order them with **Move Up** and **Move Down**, then pick a **Filter**, **Group By** and **Sort By**.
4. **Report Title**: the title, header and footer, and **Show Grand Totals**.
5. **Run and Save**: under **Send Report To** pick Screen, HTML, Excel, PDF or CSV file, click **Run Report...**, and type a **Report Name** and click **Save Report** to keep it.

## Good to know
> A report sent to Screen opens in its own tab beside the other views.
> Only a saved report shows in the table; give it a **Report Name** and save it to run it again later.
> **Finish** saves the report if it has a name, then runs it.
