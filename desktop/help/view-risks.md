# Risks

The Risks view is the open project's risk register: the things that could go wrong (threats) or go better than planned (opportunities), who owns each one and what is being done about it. Open it with `Project > Risks` or the **Risks** button on the Project bar.

## What it shows
- **Layout: Risks**: the strip above the table. Click it to **Expand All**, **Collapse All**, or show or hide the details pane with **Details**.
- **Risk table**: one row per risk with **Risk ID**, **Risk Name**, **Risk Category**, **Risk Type**, **Risk Status**, **Risk Owner**, **Identified On**, **Identified By** and **Response Type**.
- **Command bar**: **Add** and **Delete** on the right edge.
- **Risk Details**: tabs for the selected risk.
- **General**: ID, name, category, type (**Threat** or **Opportunity**), status, owner, who identified it and when.
- **Impact**: probability, schedule and cost values before the response (**Pre-Response**) and after it (**Post-Response**), the **Response Type** (such as Accept, Avoid, Transfer or Reduce) and a **Response Description**.
- **Activities**: the activities the risk could affect, with their start and finish. **Assign** adds one, **Remove** takes the selected one off.
- **Description**, **Cause**, **Effect** and **Notes**: free text.

## Common tasks
1. Click **Add** (or press `Insert`). A new risk starts as a Proposed Threat with an ID such as RISK-1, identified today.
2. Name it and set its category, status and owner on the **General** tab.
3. Score it on the **Impact** tab and choose a response.
4. On **Activities**, click **Assign** and pick the activities it touches.

Right-click a row for **Add**, **Delete**, **Risk Details** and **Hide Detail Window**.

## Good to know
> **Customize Columns...** on the right-click menu picks and orders the columns; the choice is kept with the view layout.
> Deleting a risk also removes its activity assignments.
> The statuses are Proposed, Open and Active, plus three that mean the risk is closed: Rejected, Impacted and Managed.
> Many XER files carry no risk register. The view is then empty, and the first **Add** creates one.
> Risks do not change the schedule dates; they are a record for planning and review.
